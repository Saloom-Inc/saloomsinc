    On a phoneUse the on-screen buttons. Landscape works best.
    1. Collect science. Carry rock samples, core tubes and film back to the lander and load them.
    2. Set up experiments. Carry each instrument to its marked site and press E. Instruments score by being left behind.
    3. Lighten the load. Your cabin starts full of gear. Throw out what you don't need, or you can't lift off.
    4. Watch your oxygen. When it runs out, you launch with whatever is on board.

    About

    Leave It Behind is a game built for the NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026 challenge "Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars."

    NASA's History Office counts hundreds of human-made objects on the Moon. Most are there because astronauts had to choose between carrying gear home and carrying science home. This game lets you make that choice yourself.

    Weights are simplified game units, not real Apollo masses. Mission facts come from NASA's History Office catalogue and the NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive. Not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.
